# The Remote Viewer Network

A sovereign, local-first, zero-trust network — social, communications,
education, and safety in one native app, built by one founder.
Everything runs on your device. No cloud, no tracking, no backdoors.

## Download & install

The app is public. Get it from the
[Releases page](https://github.com/Sentinel-Architech/The-Remote-Viewer-Native/releases):

1. Open the latest release and download the APK (current build: TRV-debug-v12.apk).
2. Tap the downloaded file and install. If the app is already installed, it
   updates in place. If your phone asks, allow installs from your browser or
   Files app.
3. Open "The Remote Viewer Network".

No Play Store, no Google account, no terminal. Works on Android 5.0 and up —
phones, tablets, Chromebooks, and GrapheneOS. Verified running in the field
on a Pixel 7 (GrapheneOS) and a Pixel 6a on launch night, Oct 20, 2026.

> **Field note, launch night — Oct 20, 2026:** the architect's home WiFi
> can't stay up for one evening — which is why TRV is required, by rule, to
> sustain WiFi resets. The people TRV is built for are the ones whose
> connections drop.

## Principles

- **Native stack only.** No web wrappers, no corporate SDKs.
- **Zero bloat, zero corporate, zero backdoors.** 100% open-source,
  minimal dependencies.
- **Everything on-device.** Your data stays on your device.
- **Encrypted per individual Remote Viewer.**
- **The user retains full ownership.** No human may claim ownership
  because their AI helped create an aspect.
